Delivery Policy

MES Architect · last updated 18 November 2026

Everything we sell is digital. There's nothing to ship and no delivery charge. Your purchase opens in your MES Architect account, usually within a minute of paying.

What you receive

The paid modules of MES Architect, or the full bundle. That means the online lessons, the mock interview and quiz mode, and the AI Interview Coach. We don't sell or send any physical products.

How you get it

  • Sign in, or create a free account, and buy from inside the app. As soon as the payment is confirmed, the module opens in your account. That usually takes under a minute, and at most a few minutes if the payment network is slow.
  • You can use it on the website and in the Android app, on up to 2 devices at a time. Just sign in with the same email.
  • It works anywhere in the world where you can open the website or the app.
  • Razorpay emails you a receipt for the payment.

If your purchase doesn't show up

  1. Check that you're signed in with the same email you paid with, and that you've verified it.
  2. Open the menu, go to Your account, and tap Restore purchases.
  3. Paid with a different email? Tap Paid with another email? in the same place and enter the payment ID from your receipt.
  4. Still not there after 24 hours? Email primeatnit@gmail.com with your payment ID. We'll sort it out within 2 business days, or refund you in full if we can't.
