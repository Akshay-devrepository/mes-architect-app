Privacy Policy

MES Architect · last updated 29 October 2026

The short version

No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. Your progress, bookmarks and unlocked modules are stored on your device. Data leaves your device only when you use a feature that needs it (validating a license key, the AI coach, a signed-in account, a purchase, the Android app's update check) and only to the service that feature uses, listed below.

Stored on your device

In your browser's or the app's local storage, and nowhere else:

  • Learning progress, last-visited module, quiz schedule, bookmarks and text size.
  • Modules you have unlocked, and the license key(s) that unlocked them, so they work offline.
  • A random device ID (not derived from your hardware), used only for license activation.
  • The last few error messages from the app, so a bug report can include them. They are only sent if you send feedback, and you see them in the email before sending.
  • If you sign in: your email address and a sign-in session token.

Clearing the app's data (or your browser's site data) erases all of it.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

Feedback emails

"Feedback / Report an issue" opens your own email app with a pre-filled message (app version, current screen, recent error messages). Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

Retention and deletion

  • Delete your account from the app's sidebar (Your account → Delete account). This erases your sign-in and usage history and signs the device out.
  • Purchase records (email, order ID, product, amount, date) are kept because tax and consumer law require it, and so refunds and chargebacks can be handled.
  • Everything stored on your device is removed by clearing the app's data.
  • For anything else, or to ask what we hold about you, email us (below). We reply within 30 days.
