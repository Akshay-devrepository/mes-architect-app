Refund & Cancellation

MES Architect · last updated 18 November 2026

Changed your mind? Just ask within 7 days of buying and we'll refund you in full. You don't need to give a reason.

Refunds within 7 days

  • This covers everything we sell, whether it's one module or the full bundle.
  • To ask for a refund, email primeatnit@gmail.com with [Refund] in the subject. Send it from the email you use for MES Architect, or tell us which email that is. If you have your Razorpay receipt, the payment ID (it starts with pay_) helps us find it faster.
  • We'll reply within 2 business days and start the refund the same day.
  • The money goes back to the card, UPI or bank account you paid with. Banks usually take 5 to 7 business days to show it.
  • Once the refund goes through, the module (or the bundle) is removed from your account on all your devices.

After 7 days

We don't refund purchases after 7 days, with two exceptions. We'll always refund you, at any time, if:

  • you were charged twice, or charged for something you already had, or
  • something you bought doesn't work and we can't fix it in a reasonable time.

If a payment fails

Sometimes money leaves your account even though the payment didn't go through. When that happens, Razorpay and your bank send it back on their own, usually within 5 to 7 business days. If your payment worked but the module isn't showing up, the Delivery Policy explains what to do. Most of the time it's fixed by signing in with the email you paid with, or tapping "Restore purchases".

Cancelling

  • Every purchase is a single payment. There's no subscription to cancel, and you'll never be charged again unless you buy something new.
  • Modules open as soon as you pay, so an order can't be cancelled afterwards. Ask for a refund instead. Within 7 days, that's always fine.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the app's menu (Your account, then Delete account). Deleting it doesn't refund anything, so if you're within 7 days and want your money back, ask for the refund first.

Before you dispute a charge

Please write to us first. A refund from us is quicker and easier than going through your bank. While a dispute is open, access to that purchase is paused.
