// MODULE 01 · ROADMAP
Learning Roadmap to MES Architect
30-day, 90-day, and 6-month structured learning paths with daily study plans, hands-on projects, and mock interview milestones.

30-Day Sprint — Foundation & Interview Prep

WeekFocusDaily ActivitiesMilestone
Week 1 ISA-95 & ISA-88 Mastery Read ISA-95 Part 1-2, map equipment hierarchy for your current plant, practice explaining to a non-technical colleague Can whiteboard ISA-95 levels and object models in 10 min
Week 2 Enterprise Architecture Draw the IT/OT stack for 3 industries (pharma, auto, F&B), learn OPC-UA vs MQTT, study integration patterns Can design a multi-site MES architecture on a whiteboard
Week 3 Cloud & Migration Azure fundamentals (AZ-900 or equivalent), study IoT Hub, Event Hub, AKS basics, draw cloud MES architecture Can explain cloud MES migration strategy with trade-offs
Week 4 Mock Interviews Daily 30-min mock with AI coach, STAR format responses, executive communication practice, technical Q&A Confident in all three interview modes: technical, scenario, behavioural

90-Day Plan — Architect-Level Depth

Month 1 — Core Concepts
  • ISA-95 Parts 1-5 in depth
  • ISA-88 batch execution mastery
  • All MES modules end-to-end
  • Hands-on: Ignition free trial — configure OEE dashboard
  • Project: Draw full ISA-95 hierarchy for your industry
Month 2 — Architecture
  • Cloud platforms: Azure IoT/AWS IoT
  • MQTT + Sparkplug B protocol lab
  • OPC-UA simulator setup
  • Node-RED flow for OT data collection
  • Docker + K8s basics (Kubernetes playground)
  • Project: Design cloud MES architecture for a mid-sized plant
Month 3 — Consulting Skills
  • Read: "The Trusted Advisor" by Maister
  • Practice workshop facilitation techniques
  • Executive presentation preparation
  • 20+ mock interviews with AI coach
  • Project: Full MES business case for a hypothetical client

6-Month Plan — Complete MES Architect

1-2
Months 1-2: Standards & Fundamentals

ISA-95, ISA-88, MES modules, industry-specific knowledge. Build your personal reference architecture library.

3-4
Months 3-4: Architecture & Technology

Cloud platforms, edge computing, integration patterns, UNS/MQTT. Get Azure IoT Developer or AWS IoT certified. Build a home lab with Ignition Edge + Node-RED + MQTT.

5
Month 5: Consulting & Business

Business case development, ROI modeling, workshop facilitation, executive communication, proposal writing. Shadow or participate in a real client discovery call if possible.

6
Month 6: Interview Ready

Weekly mock interviews, portfolio of case studies and architecture artifacts, Certified Manufacturing Engineer (CMfgE) or CPIM certification pursuit, LinkedIn profile with thought leadership content.
